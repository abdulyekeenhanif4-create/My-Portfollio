About Me

Design‑driven front‑end experiences

Bridging the gap between product thinking and implementation to craft interfaces that are both beautiful and functional.

I am Abdul Yekeen Hanif, a UI/UX designer and web developer focused on building clear, intuitive experiences for the web. My work blends a strong visual sense with thoughtful interaction design, ensuring that every screen has a purpose and every detail supports the user’s journey.

From early discovery and wireframes, through high‑fidelity design in Figma, to responsive front‑end implementation with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, I enjoy owning the full flow. I care deeply about usability, accessibility, and systems that scale.

Whether you need a polished marketing site, a data‑heavy dashboard, or a consistent design system, I focus on collaboration, clear communication, and shipping work that moves your product forward.
